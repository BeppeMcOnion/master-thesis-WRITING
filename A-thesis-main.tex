\noindent\textbf{Abstract (English)} \\[0.5em]
This thesis approaches the problem of temporal sequences disambiguation in the context of neural network -  specifically rate neuron models - within neural populations by integrating and generalizing two previosly enstablished connectivity frameworks: one leading to fixed attractor dynamics \cite{pereira2018}, whereas the other generates sequential activity \cite{gillett2020}, even with tunable retrieval speed \cite{gillett2024}. In order to characterize the disambiguation of two different temporal sequences starting from the same initial pattern of activity, we complement numerical simulations with the analytical derivation of the Dynamic Mean Field (DMF) equations for the system via a Path Integral formulation. This approach, indeed, allows to derive also the spectrum of fluctuations around the mean field equations, from which we derive the general stability condition for the DMF states. \cite{cris&somp2018, metz2025}.

\vspace{1.5cm}
